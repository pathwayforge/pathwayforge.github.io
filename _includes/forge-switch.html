{% comment %} The collections switcher: a click on the site's title ([data-forge-title]), a right click there, a press held half a second on a touch screen, or Enter on the focused title opens the list of collections (_data/collections.yml); a click on the title again closes it. A click in the list goes to that collection's page, kept in this browser (forge-collection) so the site's address opens it next time. The title's only way in, by design. {% endcomment %}
